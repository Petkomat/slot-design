Volatility is the standard deviation of one spin’s payout per unit staked. A session figure is a random variable: its standard error falls as 1 / √spins, so a few hundred spins cannot pin down a 4.5.

    Five reels of 32 stops, as the fitter laid them out. The outlined cells are the window above; hover or tap a stop for its weight. Stop weights sum to 4096 on every reel, so every probability in the report is an integer over 40965.
